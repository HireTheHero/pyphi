Add `script/bench_louvain.py` — B3 Louvain community detection diagnostic for MIP-partition search. Builds a pairwise-MI-weighted graph from the stationary distribution, runs Louvain community detection, maps communities to matching `GeneralSetPartition` direction-variants, and evaluates those with exact GID against exhaustive `sia()`. Results written to `docs/speed_memory.md`. Key finding: Louvain fails on both benchmark networks — the micro network's true MIP is a 4-part partition (Louvain finds 2 communities), and the ring CA's uniform MI gives a single all-nodes community.
